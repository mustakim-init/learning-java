🗺️ The 4 Flowchart Shapes You Need to Know

Module 1 — Before we write code, we learn to plan with pictures!

Oval (Terminator)
Marks the beginning or end of your program.
🟢 "Start cooking" / "Serve the dish" 🔴
Rectangle (Process)
An action or step the computer performs.
🟣 "Add 2 eggs to the bowl"
Diamond (Decision)
A question with Yes/No answer. The flow splits into two paths.
🔴 "Is the water boiling?"
Parallelogram (I/O)
Input (reading data from user) or Output (showing results on screen).
🟠 "Ask user: How many eggs?" / "Display: Done!"

💡 Tip: Before writing any Java code, professional programmers always draw a flowchart first! It is like drawing a map before a road trip — you figure out every turn and decision before you start driving. This simple habit will save you hours of confusion later.
